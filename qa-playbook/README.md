# QA Playbook: Project Falcon

A Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that defines who owns what in quality and the testing principles behind the work. Created as part of the Microsoft Junior QA/Software Tester Professional Certificate (Coursera).

## Contents

- [`Project_Falcon_Quality_Playbook.pdf`](Project_Falcon_Quality_Playbook.pdf): the full playbook

## What the playbook covers

- **QA vs. QC:** QA is proactive and process-focused (preventing defects); QC is reactive and product-focused (finding defects).
- **QC responsibilities:** designing and executing test cases, logging and verifying defects, running regression tests, and reporting quality status for go/no-go release decisions.
- **QA responsibilities:** setting quality standards, reviewing requirements before development, auditing processes, and analyzing defect trends and root causes.
- **Guiding principles:** early testing, defect clustering, and the pesticide paradox, each with a concrete way it is applied in the team's workflow.

## What I practiced

- Distinguishing QA, QC, and testing roles and assigning ownership
- Applying testing principles to a realistic team process
- Writing clear, concise process documentation
